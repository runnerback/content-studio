---
title: Preview platform follows the note's platform property
---
- Switching notes now switches the preview platform automatically: the panel reads the note's frontmatter `platform` (`wechat` / `rednote` / `x`, also the toolbar labels 公众号 / 小红书 / X and common aliases such as `xiaohongshu` / `twitter`) and selects the matching mode. Notes without the property, or with an unrecognised value, keep the current mode, and the toolbar dropdown still works as before. Editing the property while the note is open applies immediately.
- New toggle at the top level of 样式设置 (next to the 公众号排版 / 小红书图卡 pages, since it applies to every platform): "按文档属性自动切换预览平台" (on by default). Its description documents the convention so other users can adopt it in their own notes.
- Tests: `tests/platform_property.test.js`, `tests/preview_mode_auto_switch.test.js`.
