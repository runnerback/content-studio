---
title: Result push-back from the Crosspost extension, extension self-check in settings
---
- **Result push-back.** With Crosspost 3.1.0+ (bridge protocol v1.1) the extension reports each platform's outcome over the bridge as it finishes. A note delivered to the extension is still recorded as `publish_status: pending` first; when the result arrives its frontmatter moves to confirmed (`platform_<name>: 1`) or to `publish_failed`, and the publishing dashboard shows the failure. Results that arrive after Obsidian was restarted are only shown as a notice and do not touch the document. Older extensions keep the 3.12.0 behaviour (pending until you check the draft box yourself).
- **Extension self-check in settings.** "Test connection" now shows, per platform, the extension's probe result (signed in, creator tab open, X draft endpoint in sync with x.com) and the last publish outcome, from the bridge's `health.adapters`. Older extensions simply show nothing extra.
- **Protocol version check.** The extension reports `capabilities.protocolVersion` on handshake; a different major version is rejected and "Test connection" asks you to update the extension or the plugin.
- Internal: `services/bridge-sync-tasks.js` maps `syncId` → note; `normalizeAdapterHealthSnapshot` in `services/wechatsync-settings.js`; new dictionary keys under `multiPlatform.health*`.
