---
title: Metered Xiaohongshu / X publishing
---
- Xiaohongshu / X draft publishing is now metered per day through the companion extension: Free 3/day, Pro 30/day, Max unlimited. Paid licenses via Afdian (redeem with order number in settings) or Lemon Squeezy (paste key).
- Settings → 其他平台: new 「许可密钥」 field, Afdian order redeem, and a live 「额度」 line with purchase link.
- Publish modal shows today's remaining quota; when exhausted, a modal offers 「升级 Pro / Max」 and 「填写许可密钥」.
- README discloses the paid tier and the license service endpoint.
