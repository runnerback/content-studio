---
title: Inline images in rednote / X cards
---
- Images under a `##` heading now render inside the same card as the text (title → image → body). Width fills the card content area, height follows the image's aspect ratio; portrait images are capped at half the content height.
- Fit check after every render: a card that cannot hold both image and text has its image split into a standalone card (same title). Cards that still overflow get a ⚠️ badge in the preview, the page indicator marks them, and export / publish shows a notice listing them.
- X publishing refuses more than 4 cards up front instead of failing at the platform.
